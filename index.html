Features

Built for the whole release process,
not just the fun part

Every tool you need to go from audio file to shareable video — without touching a timeline editor.

Beat-reactive animation

We analyze your audio file and find every beat, every drop, every energy shift. Characters bounce and glow in sync — automatically. Adjustable sensitivity so it works on a quiet acoustic track or a heavy Punjabi banger.

Lyric stamp editor

Play your song, hit pause wherever you want text to appear, type the line. It works exactly the way you'd expect — no timecodes to type, no syncing headaches.

5 lyric display styles

Karaoke, Subtitle, Float, Neon, Typewriter. Each one renders differently on screen — pick whatever matches the mood of the track.

6 visual themes

Stage spotlights, Space nebula, Retro CRT, Neon synthwave, Forest moonlight, Ocean waves. Each theme comes with its own particle system and colour palette.

Up to 5 characters

Upload PNG cutouts of yourself, your band, or any artwork. Each character gets its own dance moves and they move independently with phase offsets — it looks alive.

MP4 & WebM export

Download instantly as WebM, or convert to MP4 using FFmpeg right in the browser. Post to YouTube, Instagram Reels, or WhatsApp Status — no re-encoding needed.

How it works

From audio file to shareable video
in under 10 minutes

1

Upload your track

Drop any MP3, WAV, AAC, or OGG file — up to 20 minutes long. BeatViz automatically finds the BPM, beat positions, and energy levels. No settings to configure.

2

Add character images

Upload up to 5 photos or PNG cutouts. Transparent backgrounds work best — we'll layer them over any visual theme you pick. Your face, your logo, whatever fits.

3

Write your lyrics

Play the audio, pause whenever you want a lyric line to show up, type the text. Repeat for every line. Choose your lyric style — Neon, Karaoke, Float, Typewriter, or Subtitle.

4

Pick a theme & generate

Choose a visual style, a dance move, and hit Generate. The video records in real time (same duration as your song). Then download. Done.

Do I need to install anything?
No. BeatViz runs entirely in your browser using the Web Audio API and Canvas. Open the app in Chrome (works best there), upload your file, and you're good. Nothing to download or sign up for just to try it.
What format should my character images be?
Any format works — PNG, JPG, GIF. That said, transparent PNG cutouts (where the background is removed) look a lot better because they layer cleanly over any visual theme. If you have a JPG, tools like remove.bg can strip the background in seconds.
How does the ₹625/month subscription work?
You pay ₹625 (inclusive of 18% GST) per month. You get unlimited video generation for that month. Payment goes through Razorpay and you get a proper GST invoice by email after each payment. You can cancel at any time from your account settings — no hoops to jump through.
What happens after 12 months?
After 12 months, the subscription is automatically deactivated. You'll get an email reminder before it happens. You can resubscribe any time to continue using BeatViz — your saved projects stay in your account.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes. We use Razorpay, which is PCI-DSS compliant. We never see your card number — it goes directly from your browser to Razorpay's servers. We only store a subscription reference ID, nothing else.
