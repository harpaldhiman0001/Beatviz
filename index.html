FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install anything? +
No installation needed. BeatViz runs entirely in your browser. Just open the app and start creating. Works best in Google Chrome.
What file formats do my characters need to be? +
Any image format works — PNG, JPG, GIF. We strongly recommend transparent PNG images (character cutouts) for the best result on all backgrounds.
How does the ₹625/month subscription work? +
You pay ₹625 (inclusive of 18% GST) per month. You get unlimited video generation for that month. Payment is processed via Razorpay. You receive a GST invoice by email after each payment.
What happens after 1 year? +
After 12 months, your subscription is automatically deactivated. You'll receive an email notification and can resubscribe to continue using BeatViz.
Is my payment information secure? +
Yes. All payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We never store your card details.
