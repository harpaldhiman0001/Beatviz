Best Practices for a Better Result

1. Use high-resolution images

Blurry or low-res photos stay blurry when animated. Use images at least 1080px wide for a clean export. Phone camera photos (not WhatsApp-compressed) work well.

2. Choose transparent PNGs for characters

Cutout-style character images (no background) blend far better with animated backgrounds than square photos with a visible white or dark edge. Use remove.bg for free background removal — takes 10 seconds.

3. Match the visual style to the genre

Neon and glitch effects suit Hip Hop and EDM. Warm glow and Float style suit romantic Bollywood tracks. Sharp camera shake and Stage Lights work well for Punjabi bhangra and party anthems.

4. Keep lyrics short per line

Shorter lyric segments are easier to read on mobile screens — and most of your viewers will watch on a phone first. Break long lines into 4–6 words each.

5. Use it for every format, not just YouTube

Your MP4 download works everywhere — YouTube, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Spotify Canvas, and WhatsApp Status. Make different-length cuts from the same session.
