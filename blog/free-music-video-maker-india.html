Best Practices for a Better Result

1. Use high-resolution images

Blurry or low-res photos stay blurry when animated. Use images at least 1080px wide for a clean export.

2. Choose transparent PNGs for characters

Cutout-style character images (no background) blend far better with animated backgrounds than square photos with a visible edge.

3. Match the visual style to the genre

Neon and glitch effects suit Hip Hop and EDM; warm glow and float styles suit romantic Bollywood tracks; sharp camera shake works well for Punjabi bhangra and party anthems.

4. Keep lyrics short per line

Shorter lyric segments are easier to read on mobile screens, where most viewers will watch your video first.
